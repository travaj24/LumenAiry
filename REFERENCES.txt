lumenairy -- consolidated references
=====================================

This file collects every external reference (papers, books,
dissertations, standards, design notes) cited by the lumenairy
codebase.  Citations have been removed from inline source comments
and docstrings to keep the source clean and stable across literature
updates; this file is the single point of reference and is updated
whenever a new algorithm draws on published work.

Numbering is for in-code lookup only (e.g. ``[Ref-7]`` in a
docstring means entry 7 below).  Numbering is stable -- new entries
append to the end of each section.


Section A.  Diffraction integrals and propagation
-------------------------------------------------

[A1]  J. W. Goodman, "Introduction to Fourier Optics," 4th ed.,
      W. H. Freeman, 2017.  General reference for the
      angular-spectrum, Fresnel, Fraunhofer, and Rayleigh-Sommerfeld
      integrals.

[A2]  S. A. Collins Jr., "Lens-system diffraction integral written
      in terms of matrix optics," J. Opt. Soc. Am. 60, 1168-1177
      (1970).  Collins integral for paraxial systems.

[A3]  N. Lindlein, "Simulation of micro-optical systems including
      microlens arrays," J. Opt. A: Pure Appl. Opt. 4, S1-S9 (2002).
      Treatment of cascaded diffraction.

[A4]  L. B. Aarts, "Free-space diffraction at a circular aperture,"
      Opt. Commun. 200, 35-43 (2001).  Analytical reference for the
      on-axis intensity of a converging spherical wave through a
      circular aperture (HFPI validation reference).


Section B.  Phase-space / Maslov / asymptotic propagators
---------------------------------------------------------

[B1]  V. P. Maslov and M. V. Fedoriuk, "Semi-Classical Approximation
      in Quantum Mechanics," Reidel, 1981.  Original Maslov method
      and the Lagrangian-submanifold parametrisation.

[B2]  M. A. Alonso and G. W. Forbes, "Asymptotic estimation of the
      optical wave propagator. I. Derivation of a new method,"
      J. Opt. Soc. Am. A 15, 1329-1340 (1998).  Saddle-point
      asymptotic method for diffraction integrals.

[B3]  M. A. Alonso and G. W. Forbes, "Asymptotic estimation of the
      optical wave propagator. II. Relative validity,"
      J. Opt. Soc. Am. A 15, 1341-1354 (1998).  Convergence and
      validity analysis.

[B4]  M. A. Alonso, "Wigner functions in optics," Adv. Opt.
      Photon. 3, 272-365 (2011).  Wigner-distribution-function
      treatment of paraxial diffraction.


Section C.  Ray-based diffraction methods (HFPI / GBD / BSP)
-------------------------------------------------------------

[C1]  B. M. Mout, "Ray-based methods for simulating aberrations and
      cascaded diffraction in imaging systems," Ph.D. Dissertation,
      Delft University of Technology, 2019.  Scalar and vectorial
      Huygens-Fresnel Path Integration (HFPI) algorithm and
      validation suite.

[C2]  J. R. Mahan, N. Q. Vinh, V. X. Ho, N. B. Munir, "Monte Carlo
      Ray-Trace Diffraction Based On the Huygens-Fresnel
      Principle," arXiv:1802.08814 (2018).  MCRT-HF experimental
      validation on single-slit and circular-aperture diffraction.

[C3]  G. W. Forbes, "Beam Synthesis Propagation," Synopsys CODE V
      product documentation.  Reference for the BSP algorithmic
      approach.

[C4]  J. Ashcraft et al., "Open-source Gaussian Beamlet
      Decomposition for modeling astronomical telescopes,"
      arXiv:2106.09162 (2021).  Open-source GBD implementation.

[C5]  J. Ashcraft, "Poke: an open-source ray-based physical optics
      platform," arXiv:2309.04649 (2023).  Polarization ray tracing
      + GBD; not a runtime dependency.

[C6]  C. Hagen, "A Generalized Expression for Accelerating Beamlet
      Decomposition Simulations," arXiv:2404.12454 (2024).
      Tilted-plane analytical evaluation accelerating modified GBD.

[C7]  E. Agu and F. S. Hill Jr., "A Simple Method for Ray Tracing
      Diffraction," LNCS 2669, 336-345 (2003).  Computer-graphics
      diffraction-grating BRDF using HF principle.


Section D.  Polarization and vectorial optics
---------------------------------------------

[D1]  R. C. Jones, "A New Calculus for the Treatment of Optical
      Systems," J. Opt. Soc. Am. 31, 488-493 (1941).

[D2]  W. S. T. Lam, S. McClain, R. A. Chipman, "Three-dimensional
      polarization ray tracing calculus," Appl. Opt. 50, 2855-2865
      (2011).

[D3]  M. Born, E. Wolf, "Principles of Optics," 7th ed., Cambridge
      University Press, 1999.


Section E.  Surfaces, glasses, and prescription I/O
---------------------------------------------------

[E1]  G. W. Forbes, "Shape specification for axially symmetric
      optical surfaces," Opt. Express 15, 5218-5226 (2007).
      Q-type orthogonal polynomial surface representation.

[E2]  Schott, Ohara, Hoya optical glass catalog data.  Refractive
      index dispersion equations (Sellmeier, Schott formula).

[E3]  Zemax LLC, "Zemax OpticStudio Knowledge Base":  ``.zmx`` file
      format reference.

[E4]  Synopsys Optical Solutions Group, "Code V Reference Manual":
      ``.seq`` macro language.


Section F.  Differentiable / autograd optical modeling
------------------------------------------------------

[F1]  L. Desdoigts et al., "Differentiable Optics with dLux II,"
      arXiv:2406.08704 (2024).  JAX-based differentiable telescope
      modeling; not a runtime dependency.

[F2]  J. Bradbury et al., "JAX: composable transformations of
      Python+NumPy programs," http://github.com/google/jax (2018-).
      Optional dependency.


Section G.  RCWA and metasurface modeling
-----------------------------------------

[G1]  M. G. Moharam, T. K. Gaylord, "Rigorous coupled-wave analysis
      of planar-grating diffraction," J. Opt. Soc. Am. 71, 811-818
      (1981).  Foundation of the rcwa module.


Section H.  Diffractive optical elements (DOEs / Dammann)
---------------------------------------------------------

[H1]  H. Dammann, K. Goertler, "High-efficiency in-line
      multiple-imaging by means of multiple-phase holograms,"
      Opt. Commun. 3, 312-315 (1971).  Dammann grating design.

[H2]  M. W. Farn, "New iterative algorithm for the design of
      phase-only gratings," Proc. SPIE 1555, 34 (1991).  Iterative
      Fourier transform algorithm (IFTA).


Section I.  Standards and conventions
-------------------------------------

[I1]  Python Array API Standard, https://data-apis.org/array-api/.
      The dispatch convention used by ``lumenairy._array``.

[I2]  pyFFTW: https://github.com/pyFFTW/pyFFTW.  Default CPU FFT
      backend.


How to cite lumenairy
=====================

If you use lumenairy in published research, please cite the project
URL and the algorithms relevant to your work from the sections
above.
