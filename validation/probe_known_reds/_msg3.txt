because addition is commutative and multiplication by 0.5 is exact -- so the

Measured on WSL (numpy 2.4.6, pyFFTW 0.15.1) at n >= FFTW_MIN_SIZE only: 1.448e-15
to 1.897e-15 at n = 256 and 512 with the ping-pong on (79 % of doubles differ at
