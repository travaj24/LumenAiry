The interpreter crash (handoff section 1) -- BOUNDED, not closed.  The dense
reconstruct_field_from_beamlets loop sized its chunk from 16 bytes per output
cell per beamlet-column, a figure whose comment claimed to cover three float64
buffers and one complex128.  Measured with tracemalloc over a 64/128/192/256
grid ladder at 512 and 64 MB budgets and 512 / 1024 beamlets, the live peak is
72.0 to 96.8 bytes per cell-column: the loop overruns its own mem_budget_mb by
1.2x to 6.0x, saturating at 6.0x = 96/16, and a 512 MB budget peaked at 3 073 MB.
The sibling windowed path does this accounting correctly
(_WINDOWED_CELL_BYTES = 32.0 against ~26 measured), which is the in-repo
precedent for the fix.  Correcting the constant moves the chunk boundary, hence
the summation order, hence the output bytes on a default path, so it ships behind
DENSE_MEM_BUDGET_ACCOUNTING, defaulting to 'legacy' and byte-identical;
'measured' puts the same cell at 387 MB, under the budget, and the two arms
differ by 2.1e-17 relative.  An unrecognised value falls to 'legacy'.  NO fault
was reproduced -- what is established is that the transient is up to six times
what the caller asked for.  Whether the default flips is reserved for the
maintainer.
