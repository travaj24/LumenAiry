refactor(carrier): sweep the carrier / system / carrier_field warning chain onto caller_stacklevel(), and extend the b11 ratchet to it

Handoff 4.4, the mechanical part.  Every lens-family body was swept onto
lumenairy.elements._lens_kernels.caller_stacklevel() by WP-B11; the propagator
chain was recorded as outside that sweep and is now inside it.  No behaviour
changes except where a warning pointed at library source and now points at the
caller.

THE FAIL-BEFORE, MEASURED.  validation/probe_known_reds/probe_carrier_attribution.py
wraps warnings.warn and, for every warning raised from inside the package,
computes from the LIVE STACK both the frame the call's own stacklevel actually
names and the first frame outside the package -- so it needs no guess about how
deep any entry point is.  The decisive pair is ONE warn site reached at two
library depths: the gap_kernel='fresnel' tilt-inert notice, reached directly
through propagate_carrier_referenced (stacklevel=3 names the caller, correct)
and reached through carrier_referenced_focus_readout, which forwards gap_kernel
and tilt into the same entry point one library frame deeper (stacklevel=3 names
carrier.py itself).  2 of 4 emissions misattributed before, 0 of 4 after.

THE SWEEP.  carrier.py: 14 literal warnings.warn stacklevels plus 21 threaded
literals below them -- the signature defaults and call sites of _guard_dispose,
_warn_undeduped, _check_collins_sampling, _collins_transport,
_check_focus_containment and _check_readout_replica.  system.py: 6 (its chain
reaches the same guards from propagate_through_system and from evaluate, i.e.
at two depths).  carrier_field.py: 1 plus 2 threaded (it reports through
carrier._guard_dispose from its own entry points).  All now 0.

The three warning helpers take stacklevel=None meaning "compute it"; an explicit
integer keeps exactly its old meaning and is still counted from the calling
guard site, so nothing outside these modules changes -- _lens_real.py and
carrier_field.py call _guard_dispose without a level, and
test_niche_c14_encapsulation.py passes _warn_undeduped(..., stacklevel=1) and
still gets the frame it pinned.  _warn_undeduped counts from its own CALLER
rather than its own frame, one frame in from caller_stacklevel's counting, so
its computed default is that reading minus one; the offset is stated in the
code.  _lens_kernels imports nothing from lumenairy, so no import cycle is
introduced (verified by importing the three modules in a pinned child process).

WHY THREE MODULES AND NOT NINETEEN.  A static reachability screen
(validation/probe_known_reds/probe_stacklevel_reachability.py) flags every
propagator module, which is a possibility test rather than a measurement.  The
three swept are the ones where multi-depth reach is the documented shape AND was
measured.  The other sixteen are recorded with their census
(validation/probe_known_reds/stacklevel_census_base.json: 23 literals in
io/prescriptions_zemax.py, 9 in sources/core.py, 9 in user_library.py, 8 in
elements/pmm/stack.py, 8 in optimize/driver.py, 8 in propagators/gbd.py, ...)
as open work, because each needs its own two-caller measurement first and doing
that blind is not a mechanical change.

THE RATCHET.  test_audit2609_b11_hygiene.py: the literal-stacklevel scanner is
factored into one _literal_stacklevel_lines helper (one implementation per
kernel); test_no_literal_stacklevel_is_left_in_the_swept_lens_bodies keeps its
id and its eight files; a new sibling covers the three chain modules with the
measurement and the scope reasoning in its docstring; and a new
test_the_same_warn_site_names_the_caller_at_two_depths is the two-caller
instrument above as a test -- the same warn site reached directly and one
library frame deeper must name the caller's file in both arms.

NOT TAKEN, reserved for the maintainer (handoff 4.4's second bullet and the 4.7
list): whose diagnostic the in-glass gap-leg warnings are.  The
wave_propagator='sas' / 'fresnel' notices on apply_real_lens name _lens_real.py
because the propagator warns and its caller is the lens; making them name the
user needs the lens to catch and re-emit at its own entry point, which is a
decision about ownership of the diagnostic, not a substitution.  Deliberately
untouched.
