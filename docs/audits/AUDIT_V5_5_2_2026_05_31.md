## Part 0 — TL;DR

### The P1: multi-order field reconstruction violates energy conservation (mechanistically proven)

`RCWAResult.to_multiorder_field(...)` and `to_jones_field(..., order=(m,n))` — the new bridge that turns a rigorous solve into a propagatable JonesField (my AUDIT_V5_5_1 #1 integration recommendation) — **do not conserve energy.** The reconstructed field power disagrees with the diffraction efficiencies by **−13% to −39% (1-D) and +71% to +148% (2-D)**; the sign is geometry-dependent (the field gains *or* loses energy).

**Root cause, confirmed exactly (A1):** the solver's per-order efficiency is `R_eff[m] = Re(kz_m/kz_inc)·(|rx_m|²+|ry_m|²+|rz_m|²)/einc_sq`, but `to_multiorder_field` deposits `Σ_m(|rx_m|²+|ry_m|²)` into the field — dropping **three physical factors**: (1) the flux weight `Re(kz_m/kz_inc)` (the Poynting projection), (2) the longitudinal component `|rz_m|² = |(kx·rx+ky·ry)/kz|²` (large for steep orders), (3) the oblique-incidence normalization `einc_sq`. A1 *proved* the mechanism: reconstructing `Σ Re(kz_m/kz_inc)·|E_full|²/einc_sq` recovers `efficiencies()` to **0.00e+00** in every case; the single-order ratio is *exactly* `kz/k0`. Cross-checked 3 independent ways (real-space, direct sum, Parseval) to 6 digits.

- **It is structural, not an oblique edge case**: even the most benign config (1-D symmetric, normal incidence, vacuum, einc_sq=1) loses **16.7%**.
- **Downstream symptom (the metalens use case fails)**: because steep orders are under-weighted, the *dominant order flips* in the reconstructed field — a deflector whose true power is in order −2 reconstructs with order −1 appearing dominant. The beam deflects toward the wrong order, defeating the exact purpose the feature was built for.
- **The energy contract is entirely unpinned**: `test_multiorder_bridge` checks shapes/dtype/"finite and nonzero"/specular-reduction but **never** asserts `Σ|field|²·dx² == Σ efficiencies`. That is why a 13–148% defect ships green.

**Lead verification note (intellectual honesty):** I attempted to independently reproduce this P1 three times and each quick ad-hoc script hit a different builder-API/units error (per-polarization row structure, `incident` spec, and field-vs-efficiency normalization). My quick reproductions this cycle have been unreliable on the RCWA builder API. I therefore defer to A1's careful, 3-method-validated, mechanistically-proven result — which is authoritative precisely *because* it is a mechanism proof (recovering efficiencies to machine-zero by restoring the dropped factors), not a discrepancy observation, and because the dropped-flux-weight physics is textbook-certain (a tilted order carries power ∝ Re(kz/k0)·|amplitude|²). The P1 is real.

**Fix direction:** scale each order's tangential carrier by `sqrt(flux_m·|full|²/(einc_sq·|tang|²))` so the deposited `|ax'|²+|ay'|²` equals the order's true efficiency — OR redocument the method as the *uncalibrated tangential boundary field* (not power-conserving) with a loud caveat. Either way, add the energy-conservation assertion to `test_multiorder_bridge`.

### The P2: over-cell-shape fix is RE-BROKEN (multi-shape evasion)

The v5.5.2 over-cell-shape rejection (closing my AUDIT_V5_5_1 carry-over P3) is **per-shape only** (A2). Two disjoint disks at area-fraction 0.6 each (total 1.2) **slip through** → DC cell-average permittivity = 7.0, overshooting the shapes' own eps of 6.0 (the exact non-physical condition the fix claims to block); the solver runs and silently returns a wrong answer. A single disk of the same total area IS caught. The fix checks each shape's fraction but not the cumulative painted fraction — the chain's recurring "fix the instance, miss the composite" pattern.

### Speed + cross-connect (user asks) — a real, verified whole-library win

## Part 1 — P1: multi-order field reconstruction energy non-conservation (A1)

**Measured defects** (A1, 3-way cross-validated to 6 digits):

| Case | port | Σ efficiencies | field power | defect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1-D symmetric, normal, vacuum (most benign) | R | 0.43246 | 0.36025 | **−16.7%** |
| 1-D symmetric, normal, vacuum | T | 0.56754 | 0.49266 | **−13.2%** |
| 1-D, n_sub=1.5, normal | T | 0.87378 | 0.53509 | **−38.8%** |
| 2-D crossed grating, normal | R | 0.45386 | 1.12374 | **+147.6%** |
| 2-D crossed grating | T | 0.54614 | 0.93397 | **+71.0%** |

**Mechanism (exactly identified):** `Σ Re(kz_m/kz_inc)·|E_full|²/einc_sq` → `efficiencies()` to 0.00e+00. Single-order `to_jones_field(order=2).power = 0.6115·R[+2]`, ratio = exactly `kz/k0`.

**Downstream (the metalens failure):** deflector at 10° incidence — true dominant transmitted order is −2 (eff 0.2945); reconstructed field shows order −1 (eff 0.2702) dominant because the steep −2 order loses its `|rz|²` + flux weight. Beam deflects to the wrong order.

**Unpinned:** `test_multiorder_bridge` (lines 920-953) asserts everything *except* energy conservation. The 6 targeted pins pass green while the bug is live.

**What's correct (A1 verified):** carrier tilt = grating equation (1e-9); single-order field has uniform |amplitude| with correct phase tilt; reduction to specular is bit-identical for sub-wavelength gratings; specular `per_order_amplitudes` == `jones_reflection()` exactly; non-square + anisotropic grids handled; default evanescent masking works. So the *kinematics* (which orders, which directions) are right; the *relative weighting* (the energy) is wrong.

**Severity P1:** energy non-conservation in a field-synthesis primitive corrupts every downstream propagation/coherence calculation that consumes it, and the flagship metalens use case produces the wrong dominant order. Must fix (or loudly redocument as uncalibrated) before the bridge can be trusted.

## Part 2 — P2 + P3 findings

### P2 — over-cell-shape fix incomplete (A2)
`_validate_shapes` checks each shape's area fraction + extent, but not the **cumulative**. Two disjoint disks at fraction 0.6 each (total 1.2) pass → DC eps 7.0 (overshoots the shapes' eps of 6.0). Two fully-overlapping identical disks also slip (double-counts the form factor). `RCWAStack.add_layer(shapes=...)` evades identically. A single disk of the same total area is correctly caught. **Fix:** check cumulative/painted fraction or the resulting DC permittivity overshoot directly.

### P3 — set_blas_threads process-global thread-leak (A3)
`_BLAS_THREADS` is a process-global, not thread-local; `rcwa_blas_threads`'s save/restore is unsynchronized. Concurrent solves with different caps leak a stale non-None value (10/10 reproducible), violating the context manager's documented "restores on exit" guarantee under threads. **Physics never affected** (energy conserved; cap only changes BLAS reassociation at ~3e-15). Fix: thread-local storage or a single-thread docstring caveat.

### P3 — per_order_amplitudes flux-inconsistent + undocumented (A1)
The docstring calls these "the data the multi-order bridge is built on," but `|Ex_m|²+|Ey_m|²` ≠ efficiency — recovering efficiency requires separately applying the flux weight + `|az|²` + `einc_sq`, none documented. (The specular Jones *does* match `jones_reflection()` exactly.) Same root cause as the P1.

### P3 — evanescent/aliasing silent in to_jones_field (A1)
`_order_carrier` never inspects kz: asking for an evanescent order returns a finite, fast-oscillating bogus plane wave (no error/NaN/warning). A 4× under-sampled grid builds a wrapped/aliased carrier silently. (`to_multiorder_field`'s *default* mask correctly excludes evanescent orders; the explicit-orders and single-order paths don't guard.)

### A2-reported, LEAD-UNCONFIRMED — index-matched R-blowup
A2 reported `rcwa_efficiency_1d` returns non-physical R (up to 1e31) for large-period/low-contrast/index-matched gratings at certain n_orders (e.g. Λ=20µm, n_orders=20 → R=1.27e7, energy violated). **The lead could NOT reproduce this** across index-matched/low-contrast sweeps (Λ=20µm, n_orders 15-30, normal + oblique → R=0.09, R+T=1.0 cleanly). It may be a narrow instability at a specific config inside A2's reduction sweep, or a setup artifact. **Held as unconfirmed** pending the exact failing config — not escalated to a confirmed bug (applying the calibration lesson from the P1-A retraction: reproduce edge claims before asserting them).

## Part 5 — Whole-library speed + cross-connect roadmap (user asks, P1 agent)

### Library-wide hot-path profile (beyond RCWA)
- **#1 hotspot: the fftshift tax.** asm/fresnel/mft/vector_diffraction do 4 shifts/call (np.roll copies, single-threaded). At N=1024 shifts = 26 ms vs FFTs ~5 ms; at N=2048, 120 ms vs 33 ms. **Fix verified bit-exact (rel-err 0.0): 2.5× @512, 3.9× @1024, 4.0× @2048** on the default propagator.
- Fresnel/MFT have **no transfer-function cache** (ASM does) — rebuild meshgrids + 2 full-array `exp` per call.
- optimize's **unconditional ray-leg**: `design_optimize` runs `system_abcd`+`seidel`+`surfaces_from_prescription` on *every* evaluate, ungated; `get_glass_index` called 13×/evaluate redundantly; crashes for a non-refractive (metasurface) prescription.
- raytrace already well-vectorized.
- Infra strong (shared `array_namespace`, FFT plan cache, cache registry) — 2 genuine duplications: no shared *non-Hermitian* eig (RCWA rolls its own), and `set_blas_threads` is RCWA-local.

### Ranked cross-connect roadmap
1. **`need_ray` decoupling in `design_optimize`** (½ day) — add `needs_ray` to MeritTerm (mirror `needs_wave`), gate the ray-leg. Both a speed win AND the unblocker for RCWA-as-merit. Then register `'rcwa'` in `WAVE_PROPAGATOR_REGISTRY` → `design_optimize(MetasurfaceParameterization, [RCWAEfficiencyMerit(needs_ray=False)], wave_propagator='rcwa')`.
2. **Shift-free propagator inner loop** (½ day, bit-exact) — the 2.5–4× ASM win above; replicate to fresnel/mft.
3. **Glass-index memoization** (~2 h) — `(name, λ)`-keyed cache registered with `clear_asm_caches`; helps optimize + raytrace + propagation.
4. **Shared `backend` linalg layer** (1-2 days) — add general non-Hermitian `eig`; promote `set_blas_threads` to library-wide.
5. **RCWA → partial-coherence/Köhler** (2-3 days) — the Köhler per-angle loop already sums `angular_spectrum_propagate` over source angles; hook an RCWA Jones response per angle → broadband/extended-source metasurface response.
6. **Hybrid ray/wave/rigorous system chain** (2-3 days) — `propagate_through_system` already composes wave + rcwa elements; add a `{'type':'raytrace'}` leg via `rays_from_field` (~80% scaffolding exists).
7. Metasurface subaperture patch; 8. cross-module field reuse.

(Most of the cross-connect is *wiring existing pieces together*, not new machinery — the infrastructure is more unified than expected.)
