## 5. FINDING -- the Newton process pool has no memory clamp

**What.**  `apply_real_lens_traced(..., n_workers=K)` dispatches the Newton
inversion through `_invert_newton_parallel`
(`lumenairy/elements/_lens_traced.py:8458`), which splits the wave grid into
`K` chunks with `np.array_split` and submits all of them to a persistent
`ProcessPoolExecutor`.  There is **no memory accounting anywhere on that
path.**

**Why that bites design 121 specifically.**  The exact high-NA final leg
re-traces the last group on a fine grid sized by `_fine_trace_group_exit`.  That
grid IS memory-clamped -- `_memory_bounded_n_fine` (`carrier.py:3729`) caps it
against `_FINE_GRID_RAM_FRAC (0.5) x available RAM` using a cost model of
`_FINE_GRID_WORK_ARRAYS (4) x 16 B x n^2`.  **That model is single-process.**
The grid it approves is then handed to `apply_real_lens_traced` with the
chain's `n_workers`, and the Newton over it is forked `n_workers` ways.

At `n_fine_cap = 8192` the clamp sees 4.29 GB against tens of GB of headroom
and approves.  Measured, the 8 workers then committed **22.1 GB each**.

**Contrast, in the same library.**  `_multi_resolve_workers`
(`carrier.py:8568`) does exactly the right thing for the OTHER pool -- it
clamps `congruence_workers` against free RAM, and its comment records the
identical failure being fixed there: *"3 workers each correctly decided they
could afford a 16384^2 fine grid (17.2 GB) and then collectively asked for
123 GB of a 127 GB box -- MEASURED on design 121's fan"*.  The Newton pool
never received that treatment.  There is also a precedent for the fix inside
`apply_real_lens_traced` itself: `parallel_amp_min_free_gb = 48.0` guards the
*thread* pool that doubles the amplitude working set.

**Measured scale of the overhead.**  Serial, the same chain peaked at
**20.86 GB** for the whole process tree.  With 8 workers the pool alone
committed ~177 GB.  The per-worker figure is ~20x what its 1/8 chunk of the
6.7e7-point Newton grid accounts for, so this is per-PROCESS overhead, not the
chunk data -- which is also why serial is not merely 1/8 the total but ~1/8.5
of it.

**Cost of the workaround.**  None to the answer, and less than expected to the
clock: the handoff's own measurements put the pool at 1.10-1.53x, and the
serial acceptance run completed in 346 s wall.  `_newton_invert_chunk` is
documented and tested to preserve the serial path's numerical behaviour
exactly, so `n_workers` is a speed knob only.

**Suggested remedy (not implemented here -- `lumenairy/**` is out of scope for
this capstone):** give `_invert_newton_parallel` the `_multi_resolve_workers`
treatment -- clamp `n_cpu` by measured free RAM against a per-worker estimate,
and announce the clamp.  Failing that, at minimum make
`_memory_bounded_n_fine`'s cost model multiply by the `n_workers` the caller is
about to use.

## 7. STAGE D -- Sziklas-Siegman residual on the real 121 chain

The SS co-moving frame (`m = R_out/R_in`, `z_eff = z R/R_out`, the `1/m`
amplitude, the piston) is the one structural paraxial element left on the 121
path.  Everything else is exact: ray trace, in-glass ASM, exact-sphere carrier
reference, exact gap kernel, exact final leg.

`FIX_D4_D6_D7_2026_08_06.md` section D11 measured, against an independent
plain-numpy exact ASM of the FULL field, how far the exact kernel sits from
truth INSIDE a scaled step.  Stage D reads every SS step the real 121 chain
performs -- by spying on `carrier._carrier_step_fast`, the single site that
applies the rescale -- and maps each onto that table.  No new oracle was run.

**Per-leg reading.**

* **Steps 3-5 are free.**  Three post-DOE legs run at `m` = 1.00001, i.e.
  effectively collimated.  That is the one regime where the exact kernel is
  genuinely exact (D11: 1e-12 against the ASM oracle) and composes across

  else.  Note this is also exactly where defect D11a used to make the leg

  every leg of the real chain.

  interpolation at all -- the calibration point is the geometry.
* **Step 9 does not happen.**  With `final_leg='exact'` -- what 121's
  `na_exit` 0.405 selects -- that leg is replaced by the fine retrace + exact
  sphere + band-limited ASM Bluestein zoom, which never enters the co-moving
  frame.  Its nominal `m` would have been 0.00086 (landing essentially on the
  focus), which is precisely why the exact leg exists.
* **Step 0 is NOT covered by any total.**  `m` = 23.94 -- the source beam
  expanding from its 2.0007 mm reference over 45.9 mm to the first group -- is
  76x outside the D11 span.  A linear extrapolation of a six-point,
  non-monotone table that far past its calibration is not a bound, and this
  capstone refuses to print one.  **Closing it needs the same measurement D11
  made, at that `m`.**  That is the one open item this stage leaves.

**Chain total, calibrated steps only: 4.1e-3 (rss) to 7.9e-3 (coherent),
conservative envelope 4.8e-2 relative L2 on the envelope.**

And the direction of the trade is settled by the same rows: on every one of
them the PARAXIAL kernel is **2.0x-6.8x further** from the oracle than the
exact one (the `exact/fres` column runs 0.148-0.503).  The SS-frame residual is
real, it is the dominant remaining model error on the 121 path, and it is
strictly smaller than the correction the exact kernel buys.
