## Part 7 — F3: Physics first-principles (all 7 correct; my prompt's GBD sign premise was inverted)

**Intellectual-honesty note**: My F3 task prompt asserted the diverging-Gaussian transverse phase is `exp(-ik·rho²/2R)`. **This was sign-inverted.** F3 correctly derived from the library's `exp(-iωt)`→`exp(+ikz)` convention that a diverging beam (R>0) has `exp(+ik·rho²/2R)` (positive-going). The audit and the fix are right; my prompt's premise was wrong. The agent caught and corrected it.

### GBD F-1 full derivation (definitive)
- Library convention: `exp(-iωt)` → forward `exp(+ikz)`. Outgoing spherical wave `∝ exp(+ikr)`; at distance R, transverse phase of diverging beam = `exp(+ik·rho²/2R)`.
- Matching `exp(+0.5j·k·(1/q)·rho²)` to the diverging phase AND the `exp(-rho²/w²)` envelope gives `1/q = 1/R + iλ/(πw²)`, i.e. at waist `1/q = +i/z_R`.
- Stored `Q = -i/z_R`, so `conj(Q) = +i/z_R = 1/q_physics`. Rendered `exp(+0.5j·k·conj(Q)·rho²) = exp(+ik·rho²/2q)` = **canonical correct field**.
- Empirical (N=256, z≈1.66 z_R): ASM `[+0.212, +0.784, +1.788]` vs GBD v5.4.6 `[+0.201, +0.776, +1.769]` (matches sign + ~1%); pre-fix `[-0.201, -0.776, -1.769]` (exactly conjugate). Intensity: `max|ΔI|/max(I) = 3.9e-15`, flux ratio 1.000000000.

### Gap #4 (P3) — the persistent `1 xfailed` masks a real `create_led_source` validation gap — post-fleet auditor inspection

**Files**: `tests/unit/test_v4_15_dispatcher_pin_validate_grid_params.py::test_factory_calls_validate_grid_params[create_led_source]` (the xfail); `lumenairy/sources/core.py:1054` (the gap).

**Provenance note**: This finding is NOT a v5.4.6 sibling-gap and was not surfaced by any of the 9 fleet agents or either deep audit. It came from a direct investigation of the long-standing `1 xfailed` that appears in every release's test count (and is parsed by the V12.3 / V17.1 self-citation walkers). It is recorded here because it is a genuine — if low-severity — gap that the xfail mechanism has masked since v4.15.0.

**What the xfail is**: a meta-pin (`test_factory_calls_validate_grid_params`) asserts that *every* public source factory calls the shared `_validate_grid_params(N, dx, dtype)` validator. All factories satisfy it except `create_led_source`, which is the sole entry in `_KNOWN_VALIDATION_EXEMPTIONS` and is therefore marked `xfail(strict=True)`. This is the "ratchet" idiom: the strict xfail (a) documents the known gap as a live test, and (b) auto-fails (XPASS→failure) the day a real validator is added, forcing the exemption to be removed. It is a deliberate, well-formed fixture — **not** a forgotten or broken test.

**Why it is a real gap (the rationale does not hold)**: The exemption's stated justification (the test's own comment, `:50-54`) is that exempted factories "re-use validation through a sibling." On inspection of `create_led_source`'s body (`core.py:1054-1240`), that is **not** true for this factory: there is no `_validate_grid_params` call, no validating grid helper, and no validating sub-factory — the only `raise TypeError` sites are the legacy-positional-shim disambiguation (`:1178`, `:1206`), not grid validation. So `create_led_source` genuinely does not validate `N`/`dx` the way its siblings do; a caller passing `N=0` or a negative pitch gets a downstream array error instead of the clean up-front `ValueError` every other factory produces. The xfail honestly records a real validation inconsistency that has been deferred-and-masked since v4.15.0 — and is exactly the kind of small gap an "expected failure" can launder into invisibility (no deep audit flagged it).

**Fix**: add `_validate_grid_params(N, dx, dtype)` to the top of `create_led_source`. The xfail flips to XPASS → strict mode fails → the `_KNOWN_VALIDATION_EXEMPTIONS` entry is removed → the suite drops to a clean `0 xfailed`. Lowest-risk of the four backlog items.
