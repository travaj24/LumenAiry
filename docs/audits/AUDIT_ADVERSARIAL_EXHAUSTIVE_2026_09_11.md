## 1. Executive summary

KPI: P0=11 P1=62 P2=50 P3=24 verified=27

(The counts are consolidated table rows in §2–§14; many P2/P3 rows bundle several related defects, so the number of individual defects in the appendix reports is several times higher. "verified" counts rows the orchestrator reproduced independently of the auditor who found them.)

**Bottom line.** The library's core numerical machinery is in better shape than its size suggests: the ASM kernel, the
default `apply_real_lens` split-step model (0.85 nm rms exit OPD against an independent ray oracle), the default
`apply_real_lens_traced` pipeline, the ray tracer's OPL bookkeeping, the coordinate-break algebra, the RCWA / PMM / EME / BOR
formulations (RCWA agrees with an independent boundary-match oracle to 2e-13; PMM's spectrum matches the exact Botten roots
to 4e-13; the BOR propagating S-matrix is unitary to 1e-12), Zernike / PSF / MTF metrics, the Jones / Mueller / Berreman /
coatings conventions and the Sziklas–Siegman transform were all re-derived and re-measured against independent oracles and
found correct — often to 1e-13 or better. The defects concentrate in (a) opt-in options and sibling propagators that were
validated once on a single fixture with a phase-blind or defocus-blind metric, (b) one structural bug class — reading ray OPL
at the last surface's sag instead of the exit vertex — that recurs in seven consumers, (c) guard and cache scaffolding that
was fixed on one code path and not on its twin (NumPy vs JAX, single-cell vs stack, direct vs prepared), and (d) data / I/O
paths (glass catalogue rows, CODE V units, the designer's prescription export) where a wrong number is indistinguishable from
a right one.

### The `apply_real_lens` family — headline P1s (all measured)

* `seidel_correction=True` (recommended in the docs for doublets) makes the exit OPD 8×–3600× WORSE: three compounding defects — the fit basis contains ρ² (defocus), the ray fan is read at the sag rather than the exit vertex, and the "analytic" reference double-counts the in-glass obliquity the ASM legs already carry; the 5 nm gate passes everything and the regression test asserts a bound that cannot fail. Focus of a 115 mm-BFL doublet moves to 62 mm; a doublet's focal peak drops 27 %. ✔ (L1)
* `surface_frame=True` ("the more accurate, Zemax-style treatment") DELETES the tilt: a tilted flat face deviates the beam by 0 instead of (n−1)θ; 8 waves of missing OPD at 5 mrad on R = 50 mm; a shipped test pins the missing ramp as correct. ✔ (L2)
* `slant_correction=True` has the obliquity term with the WRONG SIGN and 1.94× the magnitude — 2.94× worse than the paraxial screen it is meant to improve; the module's own axial-translation identity, four thousand lines above, is 290–4000× better. ✔ analytic (L12)
* `fresnel=True` applies the amplitude |t|² where the library's power convention needs (n2 cosθt / n1 cosθi)|t|²: −34 % for any prescription ending in glass, −15 % for a bare cemented interface; a plate is right only because the factors telescope. ✔ (L13)
* `stop_index` out of range — including the natural `-1` — silently removes ALL aperture clipping (3.7× the transmitted energy, zero warnings). (L14)
* The `displaced` remaps (the DEFAULT routing for any decentered / tilted / freeform element) discard the input field's phase and rebuild it from a collimated congruence — a 150 mm diverging source focuses at the collimated focus. ✔ (L3)
* `apply_real_lens_maslov` reports the field on the curved exit surface as the vertex-plane field (31 waves of defocus on an f/19 singlet) and its integrand uses |J| where Van Vleck requires √|J|. ✔ (S3, S4)
* `apply_real_lens_traced`: real-dtype input crashes (T1 ✔); `newton_fit='spline'` + one vignetted ray → all-zero field (T3 ✔); `fast_analytic_phase=True` has never worked (T4 ✔); `form_error` is silently cancelled 250–800× (T5 ✔); `caustic='multibranch'` at focus → all-zero field, no warning (T2 ✔); `_reverse_prescription` leaves aspheric/tilt terms un-negated (T6).
* GBD carries the Gouy phase conjugated — and ships a public API to compensate for it (S5); `_lens_jax` runs float32 unless the caller enabled x64 (10⁶× OPD accuracy loss) and cannot be `jit`-ed on its default path (S7).
* The traced-carrier chain's focus readout sizes its stop grid from the carrier, not the beam — 4–40× low peaks when the carrier is 2–10 % off, silently (C1); `carrier_referenced_fit_radius` fits about the grid origin (C2).

### Why the suite did not catch any of this

13 319 tests (536 files, 260 KLOC, 3.65 h) collect cleanly and pass. A hand-classified random sample is 41 % contract/signature checks, 26 % code-against-itself, 26 % independent oracle. ## 15. Cross-cutting themes and recommendations

The individual findings above are numerous, but they come from a small number of root causes. Fixing the causes is more
valuable than fixing the symptoms one by one.

### 15.1 The exit-vertex bug class (one root cause, seven sites)

`raytrace.trace()` leaves every ray at its intersection with the LAST surface, i.e. at z = sag(ρ), not on the exit vertex
plane. REAL_LENS_CHANGES §3 documents this ("critical bug — now fixed") and fixed it in `apply_real_lens_traced`. Every
other consumer that reads `image_rays.opd / .x / .y` re-derives the correction by hand — or forgets it:

| consumer | corrected? | consequence |
|---|---|---|
| `_lens_traced.py` (3 sites), `_lens_jax.py` (2), `gbd.py` (1) | yes (6 hand-written copies) | — |
| `_lens_real.py` Seidel fan (L1) | **no** | ~90 µm·ρ² spurious defocus; focus of a 115 mm doublet moves to 62 mm |
| `lenses_maslov.py` (S3) | **no** | exit chart on the sag surface: 31 waves of defocus on an f/19 singlet |
| `_lens_traced_multibranch.py` (S1) | **no** | `caustic='multibranch'` P0: thousands of waves, wrapped phase on any curved-rear lens |
| `_lens_traced_uniform.py` (S1) | **no** | fold geometry resolved on the wrong surface |
| `asymptotic_canonical_fit.py` | **no** (code read) | canonical map fitted on the sag surface |
| `raytrace/ray_fan.py::opd_fan_data` (R1) | **no** | OPD fan wrong in sign and 3× in magnitude at f/4 |

**Recommendation.** Give `TraceResult` one method — `at_exit_vertex(n_exit)` applying the signed transfer
`t = −z/N; opd += n·t; x,y += (L,M)·t; z = 0` on alive rays — and make it the ONLY way a consumer reads exit OPL. Delete the
six hand-written copies. Add a regression fixture with a **curved last surface** to every ray-based propagator test: the
entire multibranch/uniform/Maslov/Seidel test corpus uses plano-rear singlets, where sag ≡ 0 and this class is invisible.

### 15.2 Validation that cannot see the defects it is meant to catch

Three phase-blind or defocus-blind validation choices let large errors through for many releases:

* **Defocus-removed OPD metrics** (the real-lens validation removes piston + tilt + DEFOCUS before reporting RMS) cannot see a
  defocus error — which is exactly what the exit-vertex class produces (L1, S3). Report the focus position (or the ρ²
  coefficient) as a separate gated number.
* **Intensity-only comparisons** (Maslov "0.17 % RMS intensity" at the output plane; multibranch "P_out/P_in") cannot see phase
  errors (S3, S5) or the |J| vs √|J| amplitude re-weighting (S4). Compare complex fields, or at least the propagated focus.
* **Flat-rear fixtures** (above) cannot see the sag class at all.
* **Self-referential oracles**: the Strehl reference is built by the code under test (A2); the imap cache is keyed without the
  flags whose bit-identity it is supposed to demonstrate (S8); tests assert strings in docstrings or the CHANGELOG (see
  TESTS-ARCH).
* **Tests that pin the defect or cannot fail**: `test_v5_2_off_axis_conic_surface_frame.py:263–268` asserts that the tilt ramp
  is GONE under `surface_frame=True` — the premise is the bug (L2); `test_audit_glass.py:92–152` wraps a phase difference and
  asserts `rms_waves < 50` on a quantity that is ≤ 0.5 by construction (L1); every `guided_modes` fixture uses Δn = 1.04, 100×
  the margin that empties the list for real fibres (H1); no test pins the SIGN of the Richards–Wolf E_z (K16) — intensity
  metrics cannot.
* **Energy closure as a convergence proxy**: on the 2-D PMM hybrid the closure improves monotonically while T00 swings 35 %
  between neighbouring truncations (G7); on a lossless RCWA structure any complex permittivity disarms the tight tripwire
  (H6). Gate on a per-order drift between consecutive truncations, not on ΣR+ΣT alone. The `TESTING_STANDARDS.md` invariant — "anything a build is entitled to move must be measured in the test" —
  should be extended to "anything the code is entitled to get wrong must be measured against something the code did not
  produce".

### 15.3 Documented-as-recommended options that are broken

`seidel_correction=True` (L1), `surface_frame=True` (L2), `slant_correction=True` (L12 — 2.9× WORSE than the screen it is
meant to improve, wrong sign), `fresnel=True` on anything not ending in air (L13), `fast_analytic_phase=True` (T4),
`newton_fit='spline'` (T3), `caustic='multibranch'` (T2, S1), `integration_method='auto'` on Maslov (S2), `carrier=<ndarray>`
(T12), the displaced remap default routing (L3), `object_distance` in `eval_image_plane_wfe` (A4), `local_quadrature` (S2),
`formulation='li'` on `pmm_jones_2d` ("validated inverse rule" — wrong sign of Im Jxx, G6) and as `PMM2DStackHybrid`'s
default (G5), `elements_per_region > 1, grade=True` ("the speed lever for TM" — a matched-DOF pessimisation, G4),
`vectorial_hfpi` ("m-theory dipole obliquity tensor" — no vector physics on the default path, K17). The pattern: an option is
added with a measured claim on one fixture, the fixture becomes the only test, and the option is never re-measured. Every
opt-in kwarg of the lens family needs at least one end-to-end oracle test on a fixture with a curved rear surface and a
non-collimated input (TESTS-ARCH lists the untested kwarg set).

### 15.4 Duplicated scaffolding is where the divergent copies live

Six exit-vertex copies; three Chebyshev Vandermonde implementations with three index orderings; four near-verbatim
Newton-intersection + Snell fan loops in `_lens_real.py` (the n_exit = 1 bug is present in exactly the two that accumulate
OPL); five `_lens_thin.py` dispatch blocks; two `is_cupy_array`s; three byte-budgeted caches with different eviction; four
prescription loaders with four schemas; NumPy/CuPy twins maintained by hand (Maslov, FGA) that have already drifted. Each
duplicate is a site where a fix lands on one side only — which is the observed history of this codebase. Consolidation
targets, in order of leverage: `_traced_common.py` (exit vertex, launch lattice, grid axis, mirror guard); one Chebyshev
module (`_math/chebyshev.py` already exists for this); one sag module (`elements/_sag.py`); one prescription schema; one
propagator dispatch helper for glass legs (which would also fix the SAS `dy` gap).

### 15.5 Caches keyed on incomplete state

`_DISPLACED_COS_GRID_CACHE` (callable identity, L5), `_DISPLACED_LUT_CACHE` (glass NAME, L6), `build_inverse_map` (omits the
flags that change the arithmetic, S8), `zernike_basis_matrix` (mid-point sample carries no X information, A7), the imap GRAM
guard priced against live free RAM (S8). Rule: a cache key must be a pure function of every input the cached computation
reads, by VALUE; anything environmental (RAM, thread count) must not change the returned physics.

### 15.6 float32 / JAX-x64 handling is inconsistent

`_lens_jax` silently runs float32 (S7); SAS cancels in float32 (K3); tilted complex64 chains promote to complex128 (C3);
`_geometric_lens_phase` accumulates a 3e4 rad piston at the default real dtype (T4 note); `rcwa/_core.py` raises and
`optimize/jax_merits.py` auto-enables — three policies in one library. Adopt the `rcwa/_core.py` policy everywhere JAX is
entered, and reduce every large piston modulo 2π in float64 before any float32 cast (the ASM already does this).

### 15.7 Comment load hides the code

Measured: `carrier.py` is 38 % code; `_lens_traced.py`'s infrastructure half is ~3 900 lines of prose to ~2 800 of code;
`detector.py` carries a 150-line docstring over a 120-line function whose surviving factor-2 bug sits in its 6 lines of maths;
several audit-trail comments now state the OPPOSITE of the code beneath them (the spline NaN "extrapolation", the `prefilter`
flag, "just inside the aperture" over `* 1.02`, "costs speed, never accuracy", the `_freq_sq_1d` D7 note). Audit history
belongs in `docs/audits/` (which already exists, 259 files) with a one-line pointer in the code; a stale comment is worse
than none because the next reader trusts it.

### 15.9 Alternative algorithms worth adopting (consolidated)

* **Ray-to-wave hand-off (Kirchhoff/ASM from the exit pupil)** for caustic planes instead of branch enumeration: exact through
  folds, cusps and the axial focus; the TR-SIBLINGS oracle (25 lines) ran 2× faster than the multibranch and 15× faster than
  the uniform path at N = 4096 and reproduced the dark-side tail the multibranch drops. This is the commercial-POP pattern.
* **Collins / ABCD-Fresnel with a Bluestein output grid** as the carrier-chain transport: exact for quadratic carriers with a
  freely chosen output pitch, removing the near-focus bridge machinery and the standoff heuristic that produces C1.
* **Route caustic work to FGA** (measured exact on free space, correct Gouy phase) and repair or retire Maslov's
  `auto → local_quadrature` path; wire the already-present `uniform_fold_airy`/`pearcey` uniform asymptotics into the saddle
  integrators (they are dead code today).
* **Spencer–Murty exact conic quadratic** in `intersection.py` (already present in `differential._adrt_step`): fixes R4, removes
  Newton for pure conics, better seed for aspheres.
* **Zernike (disc-orthogonal) basis** for the traced fits: the entire fit-radius / arbiter / predictor apparatus exists because
  a square Chebyshev basis couples marginal rays into defocus on a disc.
* **Reference-sphere OPD, Welford aspheric Seidel terms, Southwell reconstruction, Itoh/least-squares unwrap, exact-sphere
  Strehl reference, MFT-based PSF sampling** — each a few lines, each fixing a P0/P1 above.
* **WPM/tilted-plane ASM** do NOT subsume the tangent-facet ladder (RL-MODELS quantified 200–1 250 slabs per gap for the same
  accuracy); keep the screen models, use WPM only as an oracle.

### 15.10 Suggested order of work

1. Fix the exit-vertex class with one helper (15.1) — closes L1, S1, S3, R1, the canonical fit, and the uniform traces.
2. The one-character and one-line P0s: the Richards–Wolf E_z sign (K16), the `wave_opd_2d` unwrap anchor (A1), the exact-sphere
   Strehl reference (A2), a `z < 2·N·dx²/λ` guard or a frequency-domain kernel for Rayleigh–Sommerfeld (K9), the asymptotic
   `extract_linear_phase` default (Y1) — each has a measured-exact verification ready in the repro scripts.
3. The data P0s: the three glass rows (E1), CODE V `DIM` units (I1), the LA1509-C catalogue radius, the UI prescription export (U1/U2).
4. The default-path P0s in the propagators: Maslov `auto → local_quadrature` (S2) and the multibranch memory/time cliff (S9).
5. The silent-wrong opt-ins in the lens family (L2, L3, L12, L13, L14, T2–T6, S4–S7) — each needs a curved-rear,
   non-collimated oracle test — and the solver guard gaps: the JAX `PMMStack.solve` twin that skips every guard (G1), the
   `PMM2DStackHybrid` per-slot Li routing (G5), `guided_modes`' contrast-dependent margin (H1), a warning on the Rayleigh nudge (H2).
6. Cache-key completeness (15.5) and the JAX-x64 policy (15.6).
