**However, this audit identifies 2 NEW P1 + ~10 NEW P2 + ~6 NEW P3.** Most consequential: **the meta-pattern recurred AGAIN inside v4.16.0 itself**, in three new disguises that the 9 walkers don't catch. F2 surfaced the candidates and F1 found a concrete instance, two of them three-way independently confirmed.

F2's phrase: *"The pattern hasn't been retired — it has been miniaturized. v4.16.0 closes it at the public-API surface but opens it at the infrastructure-introspection surface."*

**Tier-0 v4.16.1 patch (~150 LOC, ~4 hours)** would close all 4 new P1s via: (a) update the 4 stale ROADMAP-text tests OR rewrite them to be release-agnostic; (b) convert `_clear_local_asm_caches` registration to the late-binding lambda pattern; (c) extend `_check_glass_registry_consistency()` to validate `GLASS_VALIDITY` key coverage; (d) add a `@lru_cache` ↔ `_cache_registry` enrollment walker as the 10th dispatcher meta-pin; plus the v4.16.1-already-flagged Sellmeier formula-3 evaluator.

## Part 3 — v4.16.0 mega-rollup: Bucket 2 (multi-process storage)

**SOUND** (V3). HDF5 SWMR + filelock distributed Zarr lock correctly close the v4.14.3 multi-writer data-loss gap with no measurable performance regression for realistic workloads.

- **All 15 subprocess multi-writer tests pass**. Test genuinely exercises the race condition: stubbing filelock yields 17/20 planes + `OSError: unable to lock file` crashes; with v4.16 lock yields 20/20 planes clean. The test is not just sequencing writes.
- **Backwards compatibility preserved bit-for-bit**. The v4.14.3 atomicity pin `test_v4_14_3_atomicity_pin_still_holds` passes against the v4.16 implementation. Single-process `swmr=False` callers see no behavior change. Mixed legacy/SWMR access on same file works in both directions.
- **Measured 4-writer-contended overhead** is **better than claimed**: 1.02-1.12× across grid sizes 64²-2048² (vs CHANGELOG's "~5×"). Lock overhead at 2048² is ~2%, consistent with "<5% on large planes".
- **Signature**: `append_plane_h5(..., swmr: bool = True, lock_timeout: float = 30.0)`; `_zarr_append_plane(..., lock_timeout=30.0)`. SWMR ordering correct (`libver='latest'` gated on `swmr=True`; `f.swmr_mode=True` after dataset creation).
- **filelock placement**: acquired before `h5py.File(...)`; released in `finally`; sibling `<path>.lock` file approach works for both HDF5 and Zarr (advisory lock state lives in FS layer).

**4 P2/P3 findings:** (1) SIGKILL "lock orphaned" claim is wrong on every supported platform (filelock auto-releases on process exit); (2) defensive try/except on `f.swmr_mode = True` is dead code (libver='latest' is set when swmr=True); (3) SWMR read-during-write test doesn't strictly require observable mid-flight state; (4) no test for SWMR + gzip compression specifically (works fine in practice).

## Final verdict

**v4.16.0 is the largest, most ambitious release in the series and a genuine watershed for the recurring sibling-gap meta-pattern.** With v4.15.5's V6 walker + v4.16.0's 4 new V4 walkers, the library now has 9 of 9 dispatcher meta-pins active and green. All 4 v4.16.0 feature buckets are independently strong:
- Bucket 1 walkers verified structurally sound with non-tautological counter-pins (V2)
- Bucket 2 multi-process storage performance better than claimed (V3)
- Bucket 3 optimization framework follows textbook patterns (V4)
- Bucket 4 glass catalogues verified to ~2e-7 (V5)

**However, v4.16.0 simultaneously introduced new infrastructure (`_cache_registry`, `GLASS_VALIDITY`, optional-dep extras groups) that creates new sibling-gap surfaces the existing 9 walkers don't catch.** The meta-pattern recurred AGAIN — in three new disguises (GLASS_VALIDITY matching, `@lru_cache` enrollment, optional-dep fallback uniformity). Plus a concrete instance was found: `_clear_local_asm_caches` early-binding inconsistency with the other 8 caches. Plus the most awkward finding: the v4.15 ROADMAP-content text-pin tests fail at HEAD because v4.16.0's own ROADMAP rewrite invalidated them — exactly the meta-pattern v4.16.0's structural counter-measures were designed to prevent, recurring at the ROADMAP-content-pin layer.

**Recommended:** v4.16.1 patch (~150 LOC, ~4 hours) closing the 4 P1s via (a) release-agnostic ROADMAP-pin rewrites, (b) late-binding lambda for asm_local cache registration, (c) `GLASS_VALIDITY` key-coverage assertion in `_check_glass_registry_consistency`, (d) `@lru_cache` ↔ `_cache_registry` enrollment as the 10th dispatcher meta-pin. After (d), the meta-pattern at the infrastructure-introspection surface is also structurally closed and v4.16.0's "watershed" claim becomes durable rather than provisional.

The fundamental engineering remains exceptionally strong. v4.16.0's 4-bucket rollup of v4.16+v4.17+v4.18 ROADMAP work is well-justified and well-executed. The remaining work is bounded and mostly structural / hygiene rather than physics or correctness.
