## 1. Verdict

**The EME physics is correct — one of the strongest modules in the
codebase**, with unusually honest documentation of its own limitations.
Verified by hand:

* **S-matrix primitives** (`eme_2d.py`):
  - `strip_x_modes` — the Bloch-periodic FD Helmholtz Laplacian carries
    the wrap phase `exp(±i kx0 Lx)` on exactly the right two corner
    couplings; the eigensolver routing (real-symmetric→`eigh`;
    complex-**symmetric** lossy-ε→`eig` with the bilinear
    `Φ/√(ΣΦ²)` normalisation, NOT the Hermitian norm) is correct and
    the comment's warning that `eigh` would silently discard `Im(ε)`
    is accurate.
  - `_interface` — the modal interface scattering matrix
    `(S11,S12,S21,S22) = (−(A+B)⁻¹(A−B), 2(A+B)⁻¹,
    ½[(A+B)−(A−B)(A+B)⁻¹(A−B)], (A−B)(A+B)⁻¹)` is the standard
    Whittaker/Li form.
  - `_prop` — pure propagation `S = (0, X, X, 0)`, `X = exp(i ky h)` on
    the decaying branch (bounded), the whole reason S-matrices are used.
  - **`_star`** — the Redheffer star product matches the canonical form
    coefficient-for-coefficient (`D=(I−B11 A22)⁻¹`, `F=(I−A22 B11)⁻¹`,
    the four combined blocks).
  - `cell_smatrix` cascades `prop₀·iface(0→1)·…·iface(n−1→0)` over one
    full y-period in the first strip's basis; `_global_lateral_nullspace`
    assembles field- and derivative-continuity (with the Bloch wrap
    phase on the last interface only) correctly, and the
    svdvals-for-σ / full-SVD-for-nullvector split (P3-20) is justified.
* **`mode_match`** (`eme_diffraction.py`) — I derived all four
  matching conditions (ψ and ∂ψ/∂z continuity at z=0 and z=depth,
  projected onto the plane-wave orders) from scratch; the two assembled
  rows `Ksup O(c⁺+c⁻)+O Qz(c⁺−c⁻)=2 Ksup e_inc` and
  `(O Qz−Ksub O)E c⁺−(O Qz+Ksub O)Einv c⁻=0`, and the
  `r=O(c⁺+c⁻)−e_inc` / `t=O(E c⁺+Einv c⁻)` recoveries, match the
  physics line-for-line.  The `R=(kz_sup/kz0)|r|²` /
  `T=(kz_sub/kz0)|t|²` flux efficiencies are correct.  The
  midpoint-grid plane-wave basis is exactly orthonormal for
  non-aliasing order counts (`2M+1 ≤ N`).
* **Vector Yee generator** (`_build_generator`) — I checked all four
  transverse Maxwell rows against the library's exp(−iωt) convention:
  `∇×E = i k₀μ h`, `∇×h = −i k₀ε E`, giving
  `γEx=∂xEz+i k₀μ hy`, `γEy=∂yEz−i k₀μ hx`,
  `γhx=∂xhz−i k₀ε Ey`, `γhy=∂yhz+i k₀ε Ex` — every sign matches, and
  the Yee stagger is correct (Ez reconstructed from **forward** diffs of
  h, hz from **backward** diffs of E; the transverse rows apply the
  conjugate stagger `DxB`/`DyB` to Ez and `DxF`/`DyF` to hz).  The
  `_fd_bwd = −_fd_fwd^H` adjoint pairing is the right discrete
  integration-by-parts identity.
* **`_build_generator_tensor`** — the diagonal + `exz`/`ezx` extension
  eliminates Ez from `Dz=0` correctly (`ezx Ex` coupling included) and
  **honestly documents** the residual `exz` half-node-stagger error
  (uniform-exz qz² sits ~0.3 off Berreman and does not converge in Nx —
  disclosed, deferred, with the strip generator validated independently
  via role-swapped Berreman), plus a clean `NotImplementedError` for
  `eyz`/`ezy`.

## 2. Findings

**None above nit level.**  This subsystem is rigorously correct and its
real limitations are already documented in-code as such:

* The `eme_diffraction` module is a **documented negative result**, not
  a bug: the EME real-space modes are the wrong basis for diffraction
  *efficiencies* (which are a Fourier-space-truncation notion), so a
  structured layer does not converge; the module is kept for its
  machine-precision-validated uniform-slab math and correctly directs
  efficiency users to `rcwa_efficiency_2d` / `pmm_efficiency_2d`.  The
  match *algebra* itself is exact.

### Nits
* `strip_x_modes`'s complex-symmetric `Φ/√(ΣΦ²)` normalisation divides
  by ~0 for a defective (non-diagonalisable) `A`; degenerate/lossy edge
  only.
* The `eig` path returns unsorted eigenvalues while the `eigh` path
  returns ascending; downstream `layer_modes` scans `qz²` and does not
  rely on ordering, so this is latent, but a future consumer assuming
  sorted `lam` would be surprised.
