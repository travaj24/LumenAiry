## S1.  All eight are the same disease, already named by the campaign

`PMM_FOURNAME_ADJUDICATION_2026_08_05` S3/S4 established that the modal
classification of a near-cut mode, and the conditioning of a near-cancelling
star denominator, are ENVIRONMENT FACTS -- decided by the BLAS reduction order
and the LAPACK build, not by the physics.  The four-name fix made the
wrong-cell partition dynamic for the tests that FIRE.  These eight are the
fixtures and pins that stayed hard-coded: clean-truncation calibration cells,
the conical false-positive rung, the X-1 census cells, and absolute bars
(`1e-06`, `5e-03`, `0.005`) calibrated on two environments.

Every one of them asserts a per-build fact as a universal one.  None of them is
a physics regression: on every environment measured, the DEVICE and the DEFECT
are unchanged and only WHICH CELL carries them moves.

## S2.  Name 1 -- an ordering between two numbers that are the same number

**Root cause.**  The test justified "there is no step-down" by asserting
`resid(lu) <= resid(qr)` and `resid(lu) <= resid(svd)` on ONE cond-1e14 draw.
The two residuals agree to a few percent, so the inequality is decided by
round-off:

```text
build                        resid(lu)    resid(qr)   lu/qr
Windows np2.4.4, 1/2/24 thr  9.5257e-04   9.9054e-04  0.962   <- passed
ubuntu CI, np2.2.6 .. 2.4.6  9.3063e-04   8.8442e-04  1.052   <- FAILED
```

Over 20 draws (seeds 5..9 x n = 24/40/64) x 2 thread counts the ratio runs
0.61 .. 1.32 and changes sign with the seed.  The ordering is not a fact about
the algorithms; "no direct alternative is materially better" is.

**Fix (test only).**  Score a FAMILY of 15 draws, and assert the two claims
that are decision-relevant and that hold in every cell:

```text
claim                                  worst measured   bar    headroom
resid(lu) < 2 x min(qr, colpiv-qr, svd)     1.324       2.0     1.5x
median(lu / best alternative)               1.048       1.5     1.4x
resid(ir) < resid(lu), every draw           0.942       1.0     1.06x
median(resid(ir) / resid(lu))               0.80        0.95    1.19x
```

Column-pivoted QR is now measured too (the docstring always claimed it).  The
cond-1e16 draws are deliberately excluded and the reason is recorded in the
test: past float64's reach both the ordering AND refinement stop meaning
anything (`ir/lu` = 1.014 there).

Asserts what does not depend on which cells are clean:


The cross-polarization check is the part no single ladder can give itself: at

### Name 4 (`x1_defect...`)

The parametrization is now a STARTING CELL.  If the pinned cell does not
manifest, the scan substitutes the nearest cell that does -- preferring cells
that RETURN, because X-1 is about answers that come back -- and prints the
substitution with the whole scan table.  Only if NOTHING manifests anywhere,
AND `_census_instrument_is_alive()` separately proves the screen still flags a
synthetic cond-1e20 operator, is the defect declared absent, via a structured
skip whose reason carries that probe and the table.  A dead instrument is a
FAILURE, not a skip.  The "a future fix should make this test fail" contract is
preserved verbatim: a manifesting cell that is REFUSED still fails, with a
message telling the reader to re-pin against the fix.

## S5.  Name 6 -- which rung carries the conical FP is a per-build fact

**Root cause.**  `test_t34_bar_is_REFUTED_on_the_conical_family[10]` asserted
that degree 10 fires.  It does on Windows and WSL at 1, 2 and N threads, and on
the CI image with numpy 2.2.6; it goes quiet on the CI images with numpy 2.4.6,
where degrees 12 and 14 still fire.

```text
param   Win 1/2/24   WSL 1/2   CI py3.10 (np2.2.6)   CI py3.11/3.12/3.13 (np2.4.6)
[10]    FIRES        FIRES     PASSED                FAILED (quiet)
[12]    FIRES        FIRES     -                     passed
[14]    FIRES        FIRES     PASSED                PASSED
```

Read off the census, degree 10 fires on BOTH channels locally -- channel A
`n_grow` = 1-2 on the third patterned row, channel B `spread` = 1 with
`margin` = 1.075 -- i.e. it sits one round-off-flux mode away from silence.
That is the same coin flip the four-name adjudication measured (S4) moving the
classical family's wrong-cell list.

**Fix (test only).**  The rung is a starting point; the refutation is a FAMILY
claim, and the test now adjudicates instead of asserting:

1. this rung must be RIGHT -- `|r0 / 0.133532 - 1| < 0.01`, measured 4.3e-04
   worst, 23x headroom -- and the plateau is re-measured here rather than
   quoted: the whole family agrees with the rung to 6.3e-04 against a 2e-3 bar;
2. if the rung fires, the FP is pinned there and the test is done;
3. if it does not, the family is scanned and WIDENED (degrees 16, 18); if any
   rung fires the substitution is printed with both channels per rung;
4. only if NOTHING fires anywhere does it fail -- and the failure separates
   "channel B's numerics shifted" (rungs still carry growing-forward or at-risk
   modes inside the cut's decade, but the verdict stayed quiet) from "the
   conical family is CLEAN on this build" (every rung reads `n_grow` = 0 with
   no at-risk mode), because those need different follow-ups.

Failing rather than skipping is deliberate: the conical FP is the entire reason
the T3-4 guard ships DISARMED, so its disappearance is a headline event.  The
"a future fix should make this test FAIL" contract is intact.

## S6.  Name 7 -- "the cell is supposed to be RIGHT" is a PRECONDITION

**Root cause.**  `test_t34_guard_is_silent_where_the_answer_is_right` has six
parametrizations.  Four are CURED cells (`min_feature` above M2's threshold, so
no sliver remains) and are build-independent by construction -- measured
`rel` = 0.0005 .. 0.0047, quiet, `n_grow` = 0, in every cell on both mounts at
every thread count.  Two -- `(2, 8, None)` and `(2, 6, None)` -- are on the
UNCURED ladder, whose collapse degree is exactly the per-build fact the
four-name adjudication documents.

```text
cell            Win/WSL 1/2/N   CI py3.11/12/13   CI py3.10 (np2.2.6)
(2, 6, None)    rel 0.0082      passed            PASSED
(2, 8, None)    rel 0.0057      passed            rel 4.664  <- FAILED
```

`4.664` is exactly the collapse signature this file measures at `(2, 14)` and
`(2, 16)` elsewhere; on that image the uncured ladder collapses two rungs
earlier, and the cell has migrated OUT of this control's population and INTO
the silent-wrong family that
`test_t34_guard_fires_on_every_silent_wrong_cell_of_this_build` owns.

**Fix (test only).**  A precondition that fails is RE-ESTABLISHED, not
asserted.  The control falls back down the degree ladder to the coarsest rung
that IS right on this build and makes its silence claim there.  The migrated
rung is not silently dropped: its conservation is still asserted BLIND
(`|R+T-1| < 1e-5` -- the silent-wrong signature, measured 1e-8 .. 1e-7 on every
wrong cell in every environment), so a migration caused by something OTHER than
the sliver defect still fails, and its verdict is printed.  If no rung down to
degree 6 is right, the test fails with the whole ladder.

## S9.  Name 8 -- found by the three-environment discipline, fixed here

`test_rcond_of_hsup_would_have_been_the_wrong_instrument` fails at
`OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=2` on BOTH mounts.  Its second assertion compares
`|R+T-1|` at 41 far-field orders against 61 on the SHARED (union) grid conical
staircase.  But on that device the union grid's own energy closure is a build
fact, identical at EVERY truncation:

```text

1                      6.654e-06                              1.126e+00
2                      2.135e+01                              1.796e+01
```

At two threads -- and ONLY at two, on both mounts -- the CONVERGED reference
itself misses closure by 21, so the observable carries no information about the
truncation and the comparison reads `21.3 < 1e-4 x 17.2`.  The J00 half is
unaffected: `d41/d61` = 1.6e-08 at two threads and 1.8e-08 at one.

**Fix (test only).**  The conservation claim is anchored on the DEVICE's own
baseline -- the rank-deficient truncation must ADD nothing to it (measured
equal to all printed digits at both thread counts, bar 2x) -- and the "61
breaks conservation" half is asserted where the baseline can carry it
(`base < 1e-3`: measured 1.126 against 6.654e-06, 169x headroom) with the
screen and its numbers printed where it cannot.

**REFERRED, NOT FIXED.**  The underlying reading is worse than a test bug and
is out of scope here: this shared-grid conical staircase returns a COMPLETELY
