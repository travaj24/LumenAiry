## TL;DR

**v4.14.1 closes the P0 cache-key bug and all 6 P1s from the v4.14.0 audit, plus key Tier-2 items, with strong test pins and a structurally-extensible cache-clear meta-pin that converts the "fix N, miss N+1" meta-pattern into a class-of-bug barrier.** 911 unit tests pass. Doc-drift items are mostly correctly addressed (4 retroactively corrected in v4.14.0 entry; 3 closed by code fixes themselves).

**However, this audit identifies one NEW P0 (in `glass.py`, regression from the v4.11.2 fix) and several P1 sibling-gap recurrences ON THE VERY RELEASE that shipped the meta-pin structural counter-measure:**

- **P0-NEW-1**: `glass.py:286-287` — S-LAH64 / S-LAH79 are flagged `'__sellmeier__'` in `GLASS_REGISTRY` but their entries were REMOVED from `SELLMEIER_COEFFICIENTS` in v4.11.2 (the round-3 audit found the in-code coefficients were off by ±5.8%). UI's `main_window.py:1552` references `'S-LAH64'` as a "known-good" preset.

- **P1-NEW-1**: v4.14.1's aperture=0 sentinel fix MISSED 2 of 4 call sites. `ToleranceAwareMerit._evaluate_perturbed` (`optimize/core.py:2750-2755`) and `MatchIdealSystem._make_source` (`:958-966`) both use `_cache['E_ones']` without the `mask is _ZERO_APERTURE_MASK` branch. Tolerancing runs and `MatchIdealSystem` with `aperture_diameter=0` produce a full-grid plane wave instead of zero. **Worse: `apply_perturbations` doesn't call `validate_prescription`, so the rejection-at-validation safeguard doesn't fire.**

- **P1-NEW-2**: **7 module-level `OrderedDict` caches STILL lack thread-safety locks** — v4.14.1 added locks to 3 v4.14.0-era caches but missed the older ones: `_ZERNIKE_BASIS_CACHE`, `_THROUGH_FOCUS_SCAN_JAX_CACHE`, `_PROPAGATE_SYSTEM_JAX_CACHE`, `_GS_KERNEL_CACHE`, `_ER_KERNEL_CACHE`, `_HIO_KERNEL_CACHE`, `_TRACE_JAX_CACHE`. Same race-window pattern. **F1 and F2 independently confirm.**

- **P1-NEW-3**: 4 residual `np.where(..., 0.0 + 0.0j)` array-broadcast sites NOT swept (`optimize/core.py:966`, `phase_retrieval.py:402` no `astype` recovery, `phase_retrieval.py:367` has trailing `.astype`, `psf_mtf_dock.py:230`). The CHANGELOG line 133-136 wording "final sweep" is aspirational. **V2 and F2 and F1 independently confirm `optimize/core.py:966` (3-way).**

- Plus 6 NEW P1 findings in under-examined modules (freeform XY domain guard, makedammann unit bug, `apply_rotator` missing `angle_deg=`, JonesField input validation, `create_led_source` signature drift, source factory validation gaps)

**The meta-pin structural counter-measure (`test_v4_14_1_dispatcher_pin_cache_clears.py`) is excellent design** — dynamic `pkgutil.walk_packages` discovery, pre-flight collection guard, 17 tests covering submodule→top-level and reverse direction. It DOES close the class of bug it targets. But it doesn't extend to (a) the cache↔lock pairing class, (b) the sentinel-aware-branch class, (c) recurring code-anti-patterns like `0+0j` literals. The pattern needs to extend further.

**Verdict:** v4.14.1 is **stronger than v4.14.0 on its scoped objectives** (P0 + 6 P1 cleanly closed; STRONG meta-pin; performance preserved through lock additions). But it **doesn't break the "fix N, miss N+1" pattern** — it ships its own sibling-gap recurrences in 4 places: aperture=0 fix incomplete; 7 caches still unlocked; 4 residual `0+0j` sites; cache↔lock and sentinel-aware-branch patterns not pinned. A v4.14.2 patch closing P0-NEW-1 (glass.py) + the 2 missed aperture=0 sites + locking the 7 remaining caches would be valuable.

## Part 2 — New findings (P0/P1/P2/P3)

### P0 — Silently-wrong physics (NEW)

#### P0-NEW-1 — S-LAH64 / S-LAH79 dispatch is BROKEN

**File:** `glass.py:286-287` + `:410-415`

**Mechanism:**
- v4.11.2 (round-3 audit, CRIT-3) discovered S-LAH64 / S-LAH79 in-code Sellmeier coefficients gave `n_d` off by **±5.8%** vs the Ohara catalog.
- The fix removed both glasses' entries from `SELLMEIER_COEFFICIENTS` with the intent of routing them through refractiveindex.info.
- **But `GLASS_REGISTRY` was NEVER updated** — both still flagged as `'__sellmeier__'`.
- The dispatcher at `glass.py:410-415` does:
  ```python
  if entry == '__sellmeier__':
      if glass_name not in SELLMEIER_COEFFICIENTS:
          raise ValueError(...)
  ```
- **Any prescription using S-LAH64 or S-LAH79 today raises `ValueError`.**
- `ui/main_window.py:1552` references `'S-LAH64'` as a "known-good" preset path — UI may depend on the broken glass.

**Severity:** P0 because what was previously a numerically-wrong glass (a real correctness issue v4.11.2 addressed) is now a **non-callable glass** (a hard runtime failure). The v4.11.2 fix was incomplete; that incompleteness has been carried for 3+ releases without being caught.

**Fix:** Change `GLASS_REGISTRY` entries for S-LAH64 and S-LAH79 from `'__sellmeier__'` to either the tuple form `('specs', 'OHARA', 'S-LAH64')` (refractiveindex.info routing) or delete them. ~2 LOC.

**Recommended consistency check at module load** (the meta-pattern):
```python
for name, entry in GLASS_REGISTRY.items():
    if entry == '__sellmeier__' and name not in SELLMEIER_COEFFICIENTS:
        raise RuntimeError(f"GLASS_REGISTRY drift: {name!r} flagged Sellmeier but missing coefficients")
```

### P1 — Latent bugs (NEW, NOT in v4.14.0 audit scope)

#### P1-NEW-1 — v4.14.1 aperture=0 sentinel fix MISSED 2 of 4 call sites

**Files:** `optimize/core.py:2750-2755` (`ToleranceAwareMerit._evaluate_perturbed`) and `:958-966` (`MatchIdealSystem._make_source`)

**Independently identified by F1.** The v4.14.1 P1-NEW-1 closure (CHANGELOG.md:46-66) cites "3 callers updated" (`_get_wrapper_merit_cache`, `MultiWavelengthMerit.evaluate`, `MultiFieldMerit.evaluate`). But there are **4 call sites** that consume the cache; two were missed:

**(a) `ToleranceAwareMerit._evaluate_perturbed`** at line 2750-2755:
```python
_ap = ctx.prescription.get('aperture_diameter')   # _ap == 0.0
_cache = _get_wrapper_merit_cache(ctx.N, ctx.dx, _ap, _cdtype)
E_in = _cache['E_ones'].copy()                     # full ones, regardless of sentinel!
E_exit = apply_real_lens(E_in, ...)
```
The sentinel is correctly placed in `_cache['mask']`, but `_cache['E_ones']` is always full-ones. ToleranceAwareMerit uses `E_ones` directly without the `mask is _ZERO_APERTURE_MASK` branch the other 2 sites have.

**Worse:** `validate_prescription(strict=True)` rejects `aperture_diameter=0`, but `apply_perturbations` (which generates the perturbed prescriptions) does NOT call `validate_prescription`. So perturbed prescriptions CAN have `ap=0` if the nominal is small and the perturbation drives it negative.

**(b) `MatchIdealSystem._make_source`** at line 958-966 (pre-existing bug, but same semantic class):
```python
E = np.ones((ctx.N, ctx.N), dtype=cdtype)
ap = ctx.prescription.get('aperture_diameter') if ctx.prescription else None
if ap is not None and np.isfinite(ap) and ap > 0:  # ap=0 fails ap > 0
    ... build circular mask ...
    E = np.where(mask, E, 0.0 + 0.0j)
# else: E stays full-ones (the bug)
```

**Fix:** Apply the `mask is _ZERO_APERTURE_MASK` sentinel branch in both sites, OR change `_get_wrapper_merit_cache` to also populate `E_ones` with zeros when the sentinel is in play.

#### P1-NEW-5 — `freeform.py:42-47` `surface_sag_xy_polynomial` has NO out-of-domain guard

**File:** `elements/freeform.py:42-47`

Unlike `surface_sag_chebyshev` (lines 117-135, zeros departures outside the unit box) and the Zernike branch (modes vanish outside the unit disk anyway), `surface_sag_xy_polynomial` evaluates `c * X**i * Y**j` for every pixel of the grid. For a high-order term like `(2,0): 1e3` on a 50-mm half-grid, corner produces sag `1e3 * 0.05**2 = 2.5 m` applied to pixels outside the physical aperture. Downstream raytracer sees a discontinuity at the aperture rim.

#### P1-NEW-6 — `doe.py:513-773 makedammann2d` STILL uses micrometres

**File:** `elements/doe.py:513-773`

**Known unit-convention bug from prior audits — NOT FIXED.** Defaults `periodx=61.0`, `waveln=1.31` are in micrometres while the rest of the library is SI throughout. User calling `makedammann2d(periodx=61e-6, waveln=1.31e-6)` (matching SI convention) gets a thousandfold-off `samplingx`. Returned `cell_pixel_size_x = samplingx * 1e-6` (line 754) silently rescales the output to meters, masking the input-unit drift.

#### P1-NEW-7 — `polarization.py:534-556 apply_rotator` missing `angle_deg=` kwarg

**File:** `elements/polarization.py:534-556`

API inconsistency. Every other angle-taking helper accepts `angle_deg=` (`apply_polarizer`, `apply_waveplate`, `apply_half_wave_plate`, `apply_quarter_wave_plate`). Idiomatic call `apply_rotator(field, angle_deg=45)` TypeErrors. v4.7 added the convention; `apply_rotator` was missed.

#### P1-NEW-8 — `polarization.py:96-115 JonesField.__init__` validates shape but not dx/dy/ndim

**File:** `elements/polarization.py:96-115`

Requires `Ex.shape == Ey.shape` but does NOT validate `dx > 0`, `dy > 0`, or 2-D shape. 1-D field accidentally passed in propagates through `propagate()` until the FFT raises an opaque error.

#### P1-NEW-9 — `sources/core.py:844-895 create_led_source` signature drift

**File:** `sources/core.py:844-895`

`create_led_source` has positional arguments `(N, dx, diameter, divergence_angle, wavelength, x0, y0, dtype)` — `diameter` and `divergence_angle` positional, breaking the post-v4.7 convention of keyword-only physical parameters with `wavelength` in the 3rd positional slot. Also accepts **neither `dy` nor `*` keyword-only separator**. Compare to v4.7-canonical siblings (`create_top_hat_beam`, `create_annular_beam`, `create_fiber_mode`, `create_bessel_beam`, `create_gaussian_beam`) all of which take `dy: Optional[float] = None`. v4.13.0's `dy` threading sweep missed this entire factory.

#### P1-NEW-10 — 9 of 10 `create_*` factories lack input validation

**File:** `sources/core.py` (multiple factories)

Only the DOE family (`create_diffractive_lens`, `create_kinoform`, `create_fresnel_zone_plate`) validates `N > 0`, `dx > 0`, `wavelength > 0`. ## Part 5 — Final verdict

**v4.14.1 is a substantively strong release.** It correctly closes the P0 + 6 P1s from the v4.14.0 audit. The cache-clear meta-pin is excellent design that structurally prevents one class of "fix N, miss N+1" recurrence. Locks added correctly; monkey-patch cleanly removed; performance preserved through the lock additions.

**However, the audit-meta-finding "fix N, miss N+1" recurs 4 times on v4.14.1 itself**:
1. The aperture=0 sentinel fix missed 2 of 4 call sites (P1-NEW-1)
2. The 3-cache-lock sweep missed 7 older caches (P1-NEW-2)
3. The `0+0j` literal sweep missed 4 array-broadcast sites (P1-NEW-4)
4. The `clear_asm_caches` chain covers 2 of 7+ caches (P1-NEW-3)

Plus a **P0 carryover** from v4.11.2's incomplete fix — S-LAH64 / S-LAH79 dispatch was never fully closed, and 3 releases later it's still broken.

**Recommendation:** v4.14.2 patch closing the P0 + 4 P1-NEW items (~50 LOC, 3-4 hours). Then the v4.14 line ships clean. Future fixes should default to **shipping a meta-pin alongside any sibling-touching closure** — the v4.14.1 cache-clear meta-pin proves the pattern works.

The fundamental engineering remains strong. After 15+ audit rounds the obvious bugs are gone; what remains is in under-examined modules (where this audit found a P0 and 6 P1s) and in the meta-pattern of incomplete sibling-sweeps. Both are addressable through process gates (meta-pin proliferation, validation-coverage expansion, input-validation helper consolidation) rather than more audit rounds.
